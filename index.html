App help

For something that is not working in the app (offers, workshop status, What’s On, alerts, or newsletter), call the shop or speak to us in store. Please tell us which iPhone and iOS version you are using if you can.

If you installed a test copy through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app.

Common questions

Notifications. Shop alerts are optional. Turn them on in the app under More → Notifications, then Allow when iOS asks. Turn them off in iPhone Settings → Albion Cycles → Notifications.

Newsletter. Signup is optional. We store your email when you join. Campaigns may not send until the shop connects its mailing list. To unsubscribe, call the shop or use the link in an email once mail-outs begin.

Coupons. Reveal a unique code in Offers and show it at the counter. Scanning the QR does not spend the ticket — staff complete redemption in store.

Calendar. Add to Calendar is optional and write-only. We do not read your other events. You can refuse permission or delete the event later in the Calendar app.
