Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Albion Cycles collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you use the Albion Cycles app, redeem an offer, join the newsletter, add a shop event to your calendar, or deal with our Waverley shop. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions. If they conflict on a privacy point, this Policy prevails for that point.

1. Who we are

Albion Cycles is the independent bicycle retailer and workshop at 302 Bronte Rd, Waverley NSW 2024, Australia. In this Policy, we, us and our means Albion Cycles. You can contact us on (02) 9387 1144, using the details in the More tab of the app, in-store, or via this support site.

We handle personal information in a way that is consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), whether or not the small business exemption applies to us. If we later publish a registered entity name and ABN, that identifies the operator of the shop and does not otherwise change this Policy.

2. What we collect through the app

You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, home address or phone number to browse the app.

Installation identifier: when you generate a coupon, the app stores a random identifier in the device Keychain (and a backup copy on the device) so we can reuse the same ticket instead of creating a new one every time you open it. It is not your Apple ID, name or phone number. Reinstalling the app or using another device creates a new identifier.

Coupon records: our servers store the offer, the installation identifier, the coupon code, timestamps, expiry, and whether staff have redeemed it. Staff see enough to validate the ticket. We do not attach your real name to a coupon unless you tell staff in person.

Email: if you join the newsletter, we collect the email address you type and a record that you consented. Signups are stored so they are not lost. If we connect an email platform (for example Mailchimp), that provider also holds the address to send the list.

Notifications: if you turn shop alerts on, iOS asks for permission and Apple / Firebase may give this install a device token so we can send banners for shop rides, workshop updates and offers. We store that token against the same random installation identifier used for coupons — not your name. You can turn alerts off in iPhone Settings. Staff send alerts from the shop tools; we do not sell the token.

Calendar: Add to Calendar is optional. If you tap it, iOS may ask for write-only calendar permission so we can open Apple’s event editor already filled with that ride or event. We do not read your other calendar events. We do not upload your calendar to our servers. If you tap Add in Apple’s editor, the event is stored by the Calendar app on your phone. If you use iCloud Calendar, Apple may sync that event to your other devices under Apple’s terms. You can refuse permission, cancel without saving, delete the event later in Calendar, or turn Calendar access off in iPhone Settings.

Analytics: we may use Firebase Analytics (a Google service) for app events such as opening screens, tapping call or directions, adding an event to Calendar, or opening Instagram. This is used to understand how the app is used, not to sell a profile of you. Analytics may include device and usage information in the form Google provides.

Content you see: offers, events, news, workshop status and product images are downloaded from our cloud. That traffic is ordinary app use, not extra personal information you type.

We do not ask the app for your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. Maps and phone links open Apple’s apps when you tap them.

3. What we collect in the shop

If you leave a bicycle, buy goods, book a service, or speak to staff, we may collect your name, phone, email, bicycle details, job notes and payment details as needed to do the work and contact you. Payment card data is handled by the payment terminal or bank; we do not store full card numbers in the app.

CCTV or similar cameras, if used in the shop, are for security. Signs will be displayed where required.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to: run and improve the app; create, reuse and redeem coupons; show you shop content; send the newsletter you asked for; send notifications you opted into; add an event to your phone calendar when you ask; perform workshop jobs and retail sales; prevent coupon abuse and fraud; meet legal duties; and handle complaints.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use newsletter email for unrelated third-party marketing without your consent.

5. Who we share it with

Staff and contractors who need it to serve you (for example mechanics and people working on the app).

Service providers who process information for us: Apple (App Store, iOS, notifications, Maps if you tap through, and Calendar if you add an event); Google Firebase (database, hosting, cloud functions, analytics — our project is configured for Australia where the product allows); and, if enabled, an email platform for the newsletter. They must use the information to provide their service to us, not to market to you for their own unrelated purposes, except as their own terms describe (for example Apple’s handling of App Store data and iCloud Calendar).

Professional advisers, insurers, or authorities if the law requires it or we must protect our rights, staff or customers.

A buyer of the shop, on the basis that they continue to handle the information in line with this Policy.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some providers (including Google and Apple) store or access data outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries. Those countries may not have the same privacy laws as Australia. By using the app or giving us information, you consent to that overseas disclosure where it is needed to run the app and the shop’s connected services. We take reasonable steps to use reputable providers.

7. Storage and security

Coupon identifiers on the phone are stored in the device Keychain where available. Server records sit in our Firebase project. Calendar events you save live in Apple’s Calendar app on the device (and in iCloud if you use it), not in our Firebase project. We use industry-standard access controls for staff tools. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please tell us if you think there has been a misuse of your information.

8. How long we keep it

Coupon records are kept for as long as needed to run the offer, prevent repeat abuse, and keep shop records, then deleted or de-identified when no longer required.

Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record so we do not email you again by mistake.

We do not keep a copy of calendar events you save. They remain on your phone until you delete them in the Calendar app or remove the app’s Calendar access.

Workshop job cards and invoices are kept for as long as tax, consumer-law and record-keeping duties require (often at least five years for tax records).

Analytics are kept in line with the provider’s retention settings.

9. Your choices

You can browse much of the app without giving an email. You can refuse notifications. You can refuse Calendar access, cancel Apple’s event editor without saving, or delete a saved event in the Calendar app. You can turn Calendar access off in iPhone Settings. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter. You can delete the app; that removes the on-device identifier, but server coupon records for that identifier may remain until we purge them.

You can ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, or to correct it, by contacting us. We may need to verify it is you. We may refuse a request in the limited cases the Privacy Act allows, and we will tell you why if we do.

You can complain to us first. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

10. Children

The app is aimed at riders 16 and over, or younger riders with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect a child’s email for marketing. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change. The version in the app when you use it applies. Continued use after an update is acceptance of the updated Policy for future use.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: Albion Cycles, 302 Bronte Rd, Waverley NSW 2024, (02) 9387 1144, or as published in the More tab of the app and on this site.
