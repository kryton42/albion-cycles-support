Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Albion Cycles collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you use the Albion Cycles app, redeem an offer, join the newsletter, add a shop event to your calendar, or deal with our Waverley shop. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions. If they conflict on a privacy point, this Policy prevails for that point.

1. Who we are

Albion Cycles is the independent bicycle retailer and workshop at 302 Bronte Rd, Waverley NSW 2024, Australia. In this Policy, we, us and our means Albion Cycles. You can contact us on (02) 9387 1144, using the details in the More tab of the app, or at www.albioncycles.com.au.

We handle personal information in a way that is consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), whether or not the small business exemption applies to us. If we later publish a registered entity name and ABN, that identifies the operator of the shop and does not otherwise change this Policy.

2. What we collect through the app

You do not need an account. We do not ask for your home address or phone number to browse the app. The same shop app is available on iPhone and on Android.

Installation identifier: when you generate a coupon, the app stores a random identifier on that phone so we can reuse the same ticket instead of creating a new one every time you open it. On iPhone this is stored in the device Keychain (and a backup copy on the device). On Android it is stored in the app’s private storage on the phone. It is not your Apple ID, Google account, name or phone number. Reinstalling the app or using another device creates a new identifier.

Coupon records: our servers store the offer, the installation identifier, the coupon code, timestamps, expiry, and whether staff have redeemed it. Staff see enough to validate the ticket. We do not attach your real name to a coupon unless you tell staff in person.

Newsletter: if you join, we collect the name and email address you type and a record that you consented. Signups are stored so they are not lost. Wix Contacts and Wix Email Marketing also hold the name and address so we can send the list.

Notifications: if you turn shop alerts on, the phone asks for permission. On iPhone, Apple or Google may give this install a device token. On Android, Google may give this install a device token. We use that token to send banners for shop rides, workshop updates and offers. We store that token against the same random installation identifier used for coupons — not your name. You can turn alerts off in iPhone Settings or in Android Settings. Staff send alerts from the shop tools; we do not sell the token.

Ride morning reminders: if you opt in on a What’s On listing, the app stores the event id, title and start time on this phone and asks the phone to show a local notification that morning. We do not upload that reminder list. Cancel it on the ride screen or in More → Notifications. On iPhone you can also turn Notifications off in iPhone Settings. On Android you can turn notifications off in Android Settings. Some Android phones also have an Alarms & reminders setting, which can affect whether that morning reminder arrives on time.

Calendar: Add to Calendar is optional. We do not read your other calendar events. We do not upload your calendar to our servers. On iPhone, iOS may ask for write-only calendar permission so we can open Apple’s event editor already filled with that ride or event (title, date, time, location, address, and notes taken from the listing). If you tap Add in Apple’s editor, the event is stored by the Calendar app on your phone. If you use iCloud Calendar, Apple may sync that event to your other devices under Apple’s terms. You can refuse permission, cancel without saving, delete the event later in Calendar, or turn Calendar access off in iPhone Settings. On Android, the app opens your phone’s calendar app with those same details filled in, and Android does not ask this app for calendar permission. If you save the event, your calendar app stores it. If you use Google Calendar, Google may sync that event under Google’s terms. You can leave without saving, or delete the event later in your calendar app.

Weather: on iPhone, when a ride or event lists a start place, the app may ask Apple Weather for a forecast for that place and date. We use the address or shop location written on the listing (for example Waverley or Bowral). We do not use your phone’s GPS and we do not ask for location permission. Weather is informational only and may be unavailable for dates further ahead than Apple’s forecast window. The Android app does not request a weather forecast.

Shop ride notice: for social shop rides, the app can show a ride notice on What’s On. If you confirm you have read it, that confirmation is stored only on your device and the banner hides. You can read the same notice again in More → Shop Ride Notice. We do not collect your name or create a ride attendance list from that tap.

Analytics: the iPhone app may ask Google to record app events such as opening screens, tapping call or directions, adding an event to Calendar, or opening Instagram. This is used to understand how the app is used, not to sell a profile of you. Those records may include device and usage information in the form Google provides. The Android app in this release does not send those usage records.

Content you see: offers, events, news, workshop status and product images are downloaded from our cloud. That traffic is ordinary app use, not extra personal information you type.

We do not ask either app for your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. On iPhone, maps and phone links open Apple’s apps when you tap them. On Android, those taps open the maps app and the phone app on your device.

3. What we collect in the shop

If you leave a bicycle, buy goods, book a service, or speak to staff, we may collect your name, phone, email, bicycle details, job notes and payment details as needed to do the work and contact you. Payment card data is handled by the payment terminal or bank; we do not store full card numbers in the app.

CCTV or similar cameras, if used in the shop, are for security. Signs will be displayed where required.

4. How we use information

We use personal information to: run and improve the app; create, reuse and redeem coupons; show you shop content; show a weather forecast on iPhone for a ride or event’s listed start place; send the newsletter you asked for; send notifications you opted into; add an event to your phone calendar when you ask; perform workshop jobs and retail sales; prevent coupon abuse and fraud; meet legal duties; and handle complaints.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use newsletter email for unrelated third-party marketing without your consent.

5. Who we share it with

Staff and contractors who need it to serve you (for example mechanics and people working on the app).

Service providers who process information for us: Apple, for the iPhone app (App Store, iOS, notifications, Maps if you tap through, Weather for ride and event forecasts, and Calendar if you add an event); Google, for the Android app (Google Play, Android, notifications, Maps if you tap through, and Calendar if you save an event) and for both apps (the shop database, website hosting, the functions that create coupons and join the newsletter, and, on iPhone, app usage statistics — configured for Australia where the product allows); and Wix (newsletter name and email). They must use the information to provide their service to us, not to market to you for their own unrelated purposes, except as their own terms describe (for example Apple’s handling of App Store data and iCloud Calendar, or Google’s handling of Play and Google Calendar).

Professional advisers, insurers, or authorities if the law requires it or we must protect our rights, staff or customers.

A buyer of the shop, on the basis that they continue to handle the information in line with this Policy.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some providers (including Google, Apple and Wix) store or access data outside Australia, including in the United States and other countries. Those countries may not have the same privacy laws as Australia. By using the app or giving us information, you consent to that overseas disclosure where it is needed to run the app and the shop’s connected services. We take reasonable steps to use reputable providers.

7. Storage and security

On iPhone, coupon identifiers are stored in the device Keychain where available. On Android, they are stored in the app’s private storage on the phone. Server records sit in our Google cloud. On iPhone, calendar events you save live in Apple’s Calendar app on the device (and in iCloud if you use it), not in that cloud. On Android, a saved event lives in the calendar app you chose (and in Google Calendar if you use it), not in that cloud. We use industry-standard access controls for staff tools. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please tell us if you think there has been a misuse of your information.

8. How long we keep it

Coupon records are kept for as long as needed to run the offer, prevent repeat abuse, and keep shop records, then deleted or de-identified when no longer required.

Newsletter names and emails are kept until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record so we do not email you again by mistake.

We do not keep a copy of calendar events you save. They remain on your phone until you delete them in the Calendar app, or, on iPhone, until you remove the app’s Calendar access.

Morning ride reminders (event id, title and start time) stay on this phone until you cancel them, they pass, or you delete the app.

Workshop job cards and invoices are kept for as long as tax, consumer-law and record-keeping duties require (often at least five years for tax records).

Analytics are kept in line with the provider’s retention settings.

9. Your choices

You can browse much of the app without giving a name or email. You can refuse notifications. You can cancel a morning ride reminder on the ride screen or in More → Notifications. On iPhone you can refuse Calendar access, cancel Apple’s event editor without saving, delete a saved event in the Calendar app, or turn Calendar access off in iPhone Settings. On Android you can leave the calendar app without saving, or delete a saved event in that calendar app. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter. You can delete the app; that removes the on-device identifier, but server coupon records for that identifier may remain until we purge them.

You can ask us to delete the name and email you gave for the newsletter, and any other personal information we hold that the law does not require us to keep. Call the shop or visit us. We will delete it from our records, including the newsletter list. Coupon records we must keep for the offer or for tax stay until that duty ends, and we will tell you if we cannot delete something.

You can ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, or to correct it, by contacting us. We may need to verify it is you. We may refuse a request in the limited cases the Privacy Act allows, and we will tell you why if we do.

You can complain to us first. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

10. Children

The app is aimed at riders 16 and over, or younger riders with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect a child’s email for marketing. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date will change. The version in the app when you use it applies. Continued use after an update is acceptance of the updated Policy for future use.
